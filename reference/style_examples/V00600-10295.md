# V00600-10295 [Hogugu][Customer] Show therapists in search results during late-night hours regardless of late-night setting off

## Overview

Currently, therapists who have not configured a late-night service area are excluded from the customer app search results during late-night hours — even when they have upcoming availability the next morning or later. This limits customers' ability to discover and book therapists during off-hours browsing.

## Current Behavior

* A customer searches at 23:05 for therapists available "from now"
* A therapist has **no late-night area configured** but has a 9:00 AM slot the next day
* **Result:** The therapist does NOT appear in the search results

## Requested Change

Display therapists in the search list **regardless of whether they have a late-night service area configured**.  
But even if they have night slots enabled, as their late-night setting is off, all slots during the late-night interval will be treated as X.
