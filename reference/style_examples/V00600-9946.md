# V00600-9946 [Hogugu][Customer][NOW]Display Award Badges for 2026 First-Half Award-Winning Therapists in Customer App Profiles | HOGUGUアワードの「アワードバッジ」について

### **Description**

Based on the design provided by the partner, we would like to implement an award badge display feature on therapist profiles in the Customer App for therapists who received awards in the **first half of 2026**.

---

### **Objective**

Display award badges on therapist profile pages to recognize therapists who ranked in the 2026 H1 awards.

---

### **Scope of Implementation**

#### 1. Award Badge Display

* Display award badges on therapist profiles/therapist list in the Customer App/NOW (No need for Web app)
* Badge design will follow the artwork/design provided by the partner.
* Only therapists included in the award recipient list should have badges displayed.

---

#### 2. Display Period

* For **2026 First-Half Awards**:

    * Badge display period: From implementation date（2026/07/01 planning) until **December 31, 2026**
    

---

#### 3. Dynamic Award Text

The award text displayed on the badge should be configurable.

Examples:

* "2026 First Half South Kanto"

The text should not be hardcoded so that future award labels can be updated without requiring significant code changes.

### **Expected Behavior**

* Award-winning therapists have the appropriate badge(s) displayed on their profile.
* Award text is configurable.
* Multiple badges can be displayed when applicable.
* Badge visibility follows the configured display period.

### **Acceptance Criteria**

* Award badges are displayed on eligible therapist profiles in the Customer App/NOW.
* Badge design matches the partner-provided design.
* Display period can be configured (e.g., until December 31, 2026 for H1 awards).
* Award text is configurable and not hardcoded.
* Multiple badges can be displayed for therapists with multiple awards.
* Non-awarded therapists do not see badges.
* Verified on both iOS and Android Customer /Now Apps.
