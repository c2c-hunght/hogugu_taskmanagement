## Description (Jira raw)

## Background

Re-raising a previously discussed request. We want to change the therapist new-account creation flow.

## Requirement

1. Discontinue advance account creation by the operations team.

    * Therapists can register on their own using their phone number.
    
2. Conditions for going live:

    * Immediately after registration, the therapist is not shown in the customer app.
    * The therapist appears in the customer app only after the operations team completes review and approval.
    
3. ID verification (KYC) in the app:

    * Submitting an ID document in the app is required at registration.
    

## Purpose

Allow therapists to try the app and get a feel for how it works so they can more easily picture actually working on it. They then go through the operations review and a registration orientation session.

## Operational image

Right after registering — before the operations team's check/approval — the account is treated as "provisional registration" (仮登録) and is not displayed in the customer app. We also want to place restrictions on certain features, such as schedule registration.

Detailed requirements and specifics to be decided through discussion.
