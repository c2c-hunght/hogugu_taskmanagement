### 1. Add private coupon support to Hogugu coupons

Add a new dropdown to the Hogugu coupon management page:

`種類：プライベートクーポン／パブリッククーポン`

**Rules:**

* Once a coupon is published as private or public, the type cannot be changed.
* Coupons created before this release will default to public.

**Private coupon:**

* Operators can upload a list of customers who are allowed to use the coupon.
* The coupon is displayed only on those customers' coupon pages.|

**Public coupon:**

* All customers can use the coupon.
* If a customer is added to the list, the coupon will also appear in their My Page.
