## Description (Jira raw)

**\[Background\]**  
When a cancellation is made by the operations team via the admin panel, a notification of “Cancelled by Operations” is sent, but neither the therapist nor the customer is able to see the reason.

At present, we are handling this by sending the reason to the therapist via LINE and to the customer via email. However, we would like to make it possible to complete this within the app

On average, there are about 15 operation cancellations per month, occurring roughly once every two days.

**\[Request\]**  
Allowing the operations team to send the cancelation reason to Customer and Therapist when canceling through the admin panel.
