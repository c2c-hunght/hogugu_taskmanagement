## Description (Jira raw)

### Background

Currently, customers are required to select or enter a credit card when submitting a booking request, even if the booking is fully paid using points.

### Requirements

* Customers should be able to submit a booking request without selecting a credit card when the final payment amount is **0 after applying points and/or coupons**.

    * If the customer uses **only a coupon** and the final payment amount becomes 0, they must still select a credit card.
    * If the customer uses **points only** or **points + a coupon**, and the final payment amount becomes 0, selecting a credit card is optional.
    * Customers may still select a credit card if they want to.
    * Customers should be able to **deselect the payment method** after selecting one,
    
* If a customer makes a booking without a registered or selected credit card, any **extension menu** added during the booking should be paid for using points.
* If customer does not have enough points to cover the extension fee, the payment should result in a **payment error**.
