## Description (Jira raw)

## Overview

Send push notifications to therapists when their rank is about to change — either approaching a rank-up or at risk of a rank-down. Notifications include a deduplication window to avoid sending repeated alerts to the same therapist within a short period.

## Notification Rules

* **Delivery time:** Daily at 16:30 JST
* **Deduplication:** If a therapist has already received a rank-change notification within the last X days, do not send again
* **Thresholds:** Warning thresholds are defined per rank tier and vary by therapist rank — values to be provided by the partner

## Notification Types

| Type | Trigger Condition | Example Message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rank-up approaching | Therapist's points crossed the lower warning threshold for the next rank | "You're close to earning Silver rank — keep it up!" |
| Rank-down warning | Therapist's points dropped below the upper warning threshold for the current rank | "You're at risk of losing your rank — get back to it!" |

## Examples

**Example 1 — Rank-up approaching:**

* Silver rank requires 50 TP; warning threshold is 40 TP
* Yesterday, therapist's points crossed 40 TP
* Therapist has not received a notification in the last X days
* → Push sent: _"You're close to earning Silver rank — keep it up!"_

**Example 2 — Rank-down warning:**

* At 00:00 today, therapist's points dropped below 60 TP (rank-loss threshold)
* → Push sent: _"You're at risk of losing your rank — get back to it!"_
