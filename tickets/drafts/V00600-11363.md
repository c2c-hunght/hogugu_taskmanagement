# [Hogugu][Therapist] No therapist point (TP) for another treatment of the same customer within 12 hours | 同一カスタマーへの12時間以内の施術はセラピポイント付与対象外

## Background

* Some therapists split what should be one booking into several bookings to get more therapist points (TP), e.g.:
    * a 120-min treatment booked as 60 min + 60 min
    * a 60-min extension booked as a new booking instead of using the extension function
    * guiding the customer to use a HOGUGU coupon so that these bookings are made
* To prevent this, TP is not given for the 2nd and later treatments of the same customer × same therapist within 12 hours.

## Requirements

### 1. TP restriction

* Target: **same customer × same therapist**, **Standard and NOW bookings**.
* Reference time: the **previous treatment completion time (payment completed)**.
    * "Previous treatment" includes a **payment without treatment (未施術会計)** completion.
    * "Previous" = the most recent completion, even if that treatment itself got 0TP.
* If a treatment for the same customer is completed again **within 12 hours** of the previous treatment completion, **no TP is given** for that treatment.
    * All therapist points for that booking are not given:
        * normal TP (施術完了(新規予約) / 施術完了(リピート予約))
        * campaign bonus TP (履歴表示: 「ボーナス」 / 「ボーナス(フリー予約)」)
        * first-time repeat bonus TP (履歴表示: 「施術完了(初回限定リピートボーナス)」)
    * First-time repeat bonus: if the customer's 2nd booking with this therapist (the booking that would get the bonus) is restricted, the bonus is **lost**. Later bookings of the same customer do **not** get the first-time repeat bonus.
* Customer points are **not** affected.

### 2. Point history (Therapist App)

* A restricted treatment is shown in the TP history with **0TP**:

| App version | History display |
| --- | --- |
| New app version | `施術完了（12時間以内のため対象外）　0TP` |
| Old app versions | `施術完了(リピート予約)　0TP` |

* Other history entries are unchanged.

### 3. Rank explanation page (Therapist App: マイランク → 「ランクシステムについて」)

* In the section 「セラピストポイント(TP)の仕組み」, add one bullet:

```
・同一カスタマーへの施術は、前回の施術完了から12時間以内の場合、ポイントは付与されません
```

## Scope

* BE, Therapist iOS / Android, Creative (rank explanation page)
* **Out of scope:** customer points, commission rate, other TP rules (cancel penalties, etc.)

## Acceptance Criteria

* Same customer × same therapist, 2nd treatment completed within 12 hours of the previous completion → 0TP (no normal TP, no 「ボーナス」, no 「初回限定リピートボーナス」). Applies to Standard and NOW bookings.
* Previous completion was a payment without treatment (未施術会計) within 12 hours → 0TP.
* The 2nd booking that would get the first-time repeat bonus is restricted → no bonus; the customer's later bookings with this therapist do not get the bonus either.
* Same customer × same therapist, completed 12 hours or more after the previous completion → TP given as today.
* Different customer, or different therapist → not affected.
* Customer points for the restricted booking are given as today.
* New app: history shows `施術完了（12時間以内のため対象外）　0TP`.
* Old app: history shows `施術完了(リピート予約)　0TP`.
* The rank explanation page shows the new bullet under 「セラピストポイント(TP)の仕組み」 on iOS and Android without an app update.

## 背景・変更内容（日本語）

セラピポイントの不正獲得防止のため、付与条件の制限を追加する。

* 対象：同一カスタマー×同一セラピスト（通常予約・NOW予約の両方）
* 基準：前回の施術完了日時（決済完了）。未施術会計も前回の施術完了に含む
* 前回の施術完了日時から12時間以内に、同一カスタマーへの施術を再度完了した場合、セラピポイントは付与しない（通常TP・「ボーナス」・「初回限定リピートボーナス」すべて対象外）
* 初回限定リピートボーナスの対象となる2回目の施術が対象外となった場合、ボーナスは付与されず、以降の施術でも付与しない
* カスタマー側のポイント付与には影響なし
* 履歴：`施術完了（12時間以内のため対象外）　0TP`（旧バージョンのアプリ：`施術完了(リピート予約)　0TP`）
* 「セラピストポイント(TP)の仕組み」に追記：`・同一カスタマーへの施術は、前回の施術完了から12時間以内の場合、ポイントは付与されません`
