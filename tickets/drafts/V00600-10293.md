# V00600-10293 [Hogugu][Therapist App] Push notifications for upcoming rank changes

## Background
* Send a push to therapists when their rank is about to change: close to a **rank-up**, or at risk of a **rank-down**.
* Goal: motivate therapists to keep working and avoid unexpected rank-downs.

## Current Behavior
* No push is sent when a therapist's points approach a rank-up or rank-down threshold.

## Requirements
### 1. Trigger and timing
* Check once a day, comparing each therapist's points at **00:00 JST yesterday** and **00:00 JST today**.
* Send the push the same day at **16:30 JST**.
* **Rank-up approaching:** yesterday < notification threshold **and** today ≥ notification threshold (the therapist crossed it upward).
* **Rank-down warning:** yesterday ≥ notification threshold **and** today < notification threshold (the therapist crossed it downward), while still in the current rank.

### 2. Deduplication
* Max **1 rank-change push per therapist every 7 days** (both types together: a rank-up push also blocks a rank-down push, and the reverse).
* The 7 days must be a setting (changeable without a release).

### 3. Thresholds per rank (configurable)
* For each rank, the **rank-up notification threshold** and the **rank-down notification threshold** are **settings**, changeable without a release (same as the 7-day window in §2).
* Initial values (rule: rank-up at 80% of the next rank's minimum, rank-down at 120% of the current rank's minimum; the Silver values come from the partner):

| Rank | Min TP | Rank-up notification (to reach this rank) | Rank-down notification (while in this rank) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Regular | 0 | – | – (no rank-down) |
| Bronze | 10 | 8 TP | 12 TP |
| Silver | 50 | **40 TP** (partner) | **60 TP** (partner) |
| Gold | 150 | 120 TP | 180 TP |
| Platinum | 300 | 240 TP | 360 TP |

* Platinum: no rank-up push. Regular: no rank-down push.
* If a rank's minimum TP changes, the notification thresholds are not changed automatically; they are updated in the settings.

### 4. Push texts (JP)
Rank-up approaching:
```
タイトル：ランクアップまであと少し！
本文：{次のランク}ランクまであと{残りTP}TPです。この調子で{次のランク}ランクを目指しましょう！
```
Example: 「シルバーランクまであと10TPです。この調子でシルバーランクを目指しましょう！」

Rank-down warning (with expiring points):
```
タイトル：ランクダウンにご注意ください
本文：{失効日}に{失効TP}TPが失効予定です。このままでは{下のランク}ランクに下がる可能性があります。施術を重ねて{現在のランク}ランクを維持しましょう。
```
Rank-down warning (no expiring points in the period):
```
タイトル：ランクダウンにご注意ください
本文：{現在のランク}ランクの維持ラインに近づいています。施術を重ねて{現在のランク}ランクを維持しましょう。
```
* Rank names: レギュラー / ブロンズ / シルバー / ゴールド / プラチナ.
* "Expiring points" = the **nearest expiry within the next 30 days** (date and amount, same data as 失効予定ポイント on the My Rank page). The 30 days are a setting. If nothing expires within 30 days, the text without expiry info is used.

### 5. Behavior
* Tap → opens the **My Rank** page.
* Respects the therapist's push notification setting (no push if notifications are off).

## Examples
**Rank-up:** Silver needs 50 TP, notification at 40 TP. Points: 38 TP yesterday → 41 TP today, no rank push in the last 7 days → 16:30 push 「シルバーランクまであと9TPです。…」

**Rank-down:** Silver, notification at 60 TP. Points: 62 TP yesterday → 57 TP today; 10 TP expire on 11/20 → 16:30 push 「11/20に10TPが失効予定です。このままではブロンズランクに下がる可能性があります。…」

## Scope
* BE (daily check, push), Therapist App (iOS / Android): tap → My Rank page

## Acceptance Criteria
* Crossing the rank-up notification threshold upward (00:00 yesterday vs today) → 1 push at 16:30 JST with the rank-up text.
* Crossing the rank-down notification threshold downward → 1 push at 16:30 JST with the rank-down text (with expiry info when points expire in the period).
* Within 7 days after any rank-change push → no further rank-change push (either type).
* Platinum gets no rank-up push; Regular gets no rank-down push.
* The rank-up / rank-down notification thresholds per rank and the 7-day window are settings; changing them takes effect without a release.
* Tapping the push opens the My Rank page.
* No push when the therapist has push notifications off.

## Out of scope
* Push when the rank actually changes (rank-up / rank-down done)
* LINE / email notifications
