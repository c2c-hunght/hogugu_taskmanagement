# [Hogugu][Customer][Therapist][Dashboard][BE] Payment error handling (unpaid booking) | 決済エラー発生時の対応

## Background
* Additional to V00600-11138 (booking without credit card). 11138 defines when a payment error occurs; this ticket defines **what happens after** a payment error.
* Currently:
  * When the extension auto-charge fails after treatment completion, or the cancellation fee charge fails, there is no notice to the operator or the therapist. The therapist is paid for the full amount, including the unpaid part.
  * When the initial booking payment fails at treatment completion, the therapist cannot complete the booking (error shown in the Therapist App).

* HOGUGU pays therapists from the amount received from the customer minus the fee. The partner wants:
  * the therapist to know the booking is unpaid, and to check payment with the customer if possible
  * the therapist's reward to be **on hold** while unpaid, and paid after the customer completes payment

## Requirements

### 1. Payment error — definition
A booking is a **payment error** when an amount remains unpaid, for example:
* Initial booking payment failed at treatment completion (changed: the therapist can complete the booking; it becomes a payment error instead of showing an error and blocking completion)
* Extension on a no-card booking, not enough points (see V00600-11138)
* Extension card auto-charge failed
* Cancellation fee charge failed (有償キャンセル)

The payment error is resolved when:
* the customer completes payment from the app, or
* the operator marks the booking as paid on the Dashboard.

### 2. Operator (運営)

**2-1. Alert email** — sent to `info@hogugu.com` when a payment error occurs.
```
件名：決済エラー 予約ID#{予約ID}

エラー発生日時：{エラー発生日時}
決済金額：{決済金額}円
予約ID：{予約ID}
https://admin.hogugu.com/all-booking/{予約ID}

予約日時：{予約日時}
カスタマーID：{カスタマーID}
カスタマー：{カスタマーニックネーム}
セラピストID：{セラピストID}
セラピスト：{セラピストニックネーム}
```
* {決済金額} = unpaid amount.

**2-2. Dashboard > 予約管理**
* The operator can list payment-error bookings (add a filter of booking statuses)
* Booking detail shows the payment error and the unpaid amount.
* The operator can mark the booking as paid (e.g., paid by bank transfer).

**2-3. Reminder push to customer** — button on the booking detail (payment-error bookings only).
* Confirm dialog before sending.
* Show the last sent date/time next to the button.
* Fixed text:
```
【お支払いの確認をお願いします】
決済が完了していない予約があります。
アプリよりお支払い方法をご確認のうえ、決済を完了してください。
お支払い手続きができない場合やご不明な点がございましたら、support@hogugu.comまでご連絡くださいませ。
```

### 3. Therapist (セラピスト)

**3-1. Push notification** when a payment error occurs:
```
【決済エラーのお知らせ】
お客様によるお支払いが完了していません。
お客様へお支払い状況のご確認をお願いいたします。
予約ID：{予約ID}
```

**3-2. Until the payment error is resolved**
* Booking status is shown as **「決済エラー」** instead of 「施術完了」/「キャンセル」 (booking list and detail).
* The reward for the **whole booking** is on hold (not counted in payout). After payment is completed, it is counted in the payout.
* Therapist points for the booking are on hold. After payment is completed, they are granted.
* The therapist can chat with the customer (current: chat closes 24 hours after completion).
* The payment error does **not** affect the therapist's business: on the customer search screen the therapist is not shown as 「施術中」, and the therapist can receive new bookings as usual.

**3-3. After payment is completed**
* Status goes back to 「施術完了」 (or 「キャンセル」 for a cancellation fee).
* Reward is counted in the payout of the period in which payment is completed.
* Therapist points are granted.
* Chat closes 24 hours after payment is completed.

### 4. Customer (カスタマー)

**4-1. Until payment is completed**
* Cannot send a new booking request (app, NOW, Web booking).
* Cannot withdraw (退会).
* In both cases, show the message below and lead to the unpaid booking's detail, where the customer can register / select a card and pay.

**4-2. Messages**

New booking request blocked:
```
【お支払いが完了していない予約があります】
決済が完了していない予約があるため、新しい予約をリクエストできません。
お支払いを完了してから、もう一度お試しください。
[お支払いへ進む]
```

Withdrawal blocked:
```
【お支払いが完了していない予約があります】
決済が完了していない予約があるため、退会手続きができません。
お支払いを完了してから、もう一度お手続きください。
[お支払いへ進む]
```

* If there are several unpaid bookings, [お支払いへ進む] opens the oldest one.

**4-3. Push notification** when a payment error occurs (automatic) — same text as 2-3:
```
【お支払いの確認をお願いします】
決済が完了していない予約があります。
アプリよりお支払い方法をご確認のうえ、決済を完了してください。
お支払い手続きができない場合やご不明な点がございましたら、support@hogugu.comまでご連絡くださいませ。
```
* Tapping the push opens the unpaid booking's detail.

### 5. Not paid for a long time
* No time limit. The booking stays a payment error until the customer pays or the operator marks it as paid; the reward and therapist points stay on hold.

## Scope
* BE (booking, payout, notification, mail)
* Dashboard
* Therapist iOS / Android
* Customer iOS / Android, NOW, Web booking

## Dependency / release
* Release together with V00600-11138 (no-card bookings can create payment errors), BE before apps.
* New email template.

## Acceptance Criteria
* Initial payment fails at treatment completion → the therapist can complete the booking, and it becomes a payment error (no blocking error).
* Extension unpaid (points short / card auto-charge failed) → booking becomes a payment error.
* Cancellation fee charge failed → booking becomes a payment error.
* The operator receives the alert email at info@hogugu.com with the fields above.
* Dashboard lists payment-error bookings; the operator can send the reminder push (with confirm dialog and last sent time) and mark the booking as paid.
* Therapist receives the push; booking shows 「決済エラー」; reward and points are on hold; chat stays open.
* Therapist is not shown as 「施術中」 on the customer search screen and can receive new bookings.
* Customer receives the push when the payment error occurs; tapping it opens the unpaid booking.
* Customer cannot send a new request or withdraw; the message is shown and [お支払いへ進む] opens the unpaid booking.
* Payment completed (by customer or operator) → status back to 施術完了 / キャンセル, reward counted in payout, points granted, customer restrictions lifted.
